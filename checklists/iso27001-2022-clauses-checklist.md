# ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — management-system clauses checklist (4 to 10)

Tick each requirement when it is **implemented and evidenced**. The "Evidence" column names the document or record
an auditor will ask for. Annex A controls are covered separately in `templates/statement-of-applicability-iso27001.csv`.

Status legend: ☐ not started · ◐ in progress · ☑ done

| # | Requirement | Status | Evidence | Owner |
|---|-------------|--------|----------|-------|

| 4.3 | ISMS scope defined, documented, with boundaries and interfaces | ☐ | Scope statement | |

## Clause 6 — Planning

|---|-------------|--------|----------|-------|
| 6.1.1 | Risks and opportunities for the ISMS determined | ☐ | Risk & opportunity register | |
