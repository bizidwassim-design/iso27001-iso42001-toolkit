## 1. Purpose

This policy states [Organisation]'s commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of
the information it owns or processes, and sets the framework for the information security management system (ISMS).

## 2. Scope

Applies to all employees, contractors, interns and third parties who access [Organisation] information or systems,
and to all information assets within the ISMS scope defined in [SCOPE-001].

## 3. Policy statements

1. Information is protected according to its **classification** (Public, Internal, Confidential, Restricted) throughout its life cycle.
2. Security risks are identified, assessed and treated using the approved risk methodology; residual risk is accepted only by the designated risk owner.
3. Access is granted on a **need-to-know, least-privilege** basis and reviewed at least [quarterly]; multi-factor authentication is mandatory for remote and privileged access.
4. All users complete security awareness training at onboarding and at least annually.
5. Suspected security incidents and weaknesses are reported immediately to [security@yourcompany.example]; no one is penalised for reporting in good faith.
6. Systems are maintained securely: supported versions, timely patching, hardened configurations, logging and monitoring.
7. Business continuity and backup arrangements ensure recovery of critical services within agreed objectives.
8. Suppliers processing [Organisation] information are assessed and bound by contractual security requirements.
9. Legal, regulatory and contractual obligations (including data protection) are identified and met.
10. The ISMS is measured, audited and reviewed by management to drive continual improvement.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Top management | Approve this policy, provide resources, review ISMS performance |
| CISO | Own the ISMS, maintain risk register, report to management |
| Asset / risk owners | Classify assets, accept residual risk, authorise access |
| All personnel | Comply with policies, complete training, report incidents |

## 5. Compliance

Violations may result in disciplinary action up to termination, and contractual or legal consequences for third parties.
Exceptions require written approval from the CISO and the risk owner, with an expiry date, recorded in the exceptions register.
