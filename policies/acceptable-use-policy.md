## 1. Purpose and scope

Sets the rules for using [Organisation]'s information, devices, networks, cloud services and AI tools. Applies to
everyone with an account or device issued by [Organisation]. Acknowledged at onboarding and annually.

## 2. General rules

* Use company systems for business purposes; limited personal use is tolerated if it does not affect security, productivity or the organisation's reputation.
* Never share your password or MFA codes; never use your work password elsewhere.
* Lock your screen when away; keep devices with you or physically secured.
* Report lost devices, suspicious emails and anything unusual to [security@yourcompany.example] **within one hour**.
* Do not install unapproved software or browser extensions; do not disable security tools.
* Store work data only in approved locations ([file service], [collaboration suite]); not in personal cloud or email.

## 3. Email, messaging and web

* Verify unexpected requests for payments, credentials or data by a second channel (phone call to a known number).
* Do not forward company email automatically to external addresses.
* Do not access illegal, offensive or harassing content; filtering may apply.

## 4. Information handling

| Classification | Can be shared with | Storage / transmission |
|----------------|--------------------|------------------------|
| Public | Anyone | No restriction |
| Internal | Staff and contractors under NDA | Company systems only |
| Confidential | Named people with a need to know | Encrypted at rest and in transit; no personal devices |
| Restricted | Explicit owner approval, logged | Approved systems with access logging; no printing without approval |

## 5. Artificial-intelligence tools

* Use only AI tools listed in the approved register [INV-AI-001]; the approved tools are: [tool A], [tool B].
* **Never** enter Confidential or Restricted information, personal data, source code or customer data into a public AI tool.
* Check AI output before using it; you are responsible for what you send or decide based on it.
* Do not use AI to generate content that impersonates people or evades security controls.
* Say so when an AI system materially produced a document or decision, where policy or law requires.

## 6. Remote work and personal devices

* Connect through the approved remote-access service; avoid public Wi-Fi without it.
* Personal devices may access company data only via approved apps with device management enabled.

## 7. Monitoring and consequences

[Organisation] monitors systems for security and compliance in line with applicable law and the privacy notice.
Breaches may lead to disciplinary action.
